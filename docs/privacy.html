Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

The short version: Transcriber does not collect, store or send any of your data. Your audio and transcripts never leave your Mac.

Your audio and transcripts

Transcription happens entirely on your computer. The audio files you add are read locally, and the transcripts are saved as text files next to them, on your Mac. Neither is uploaded anywhere, and there is no server or account involved.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
  • No accounts, sign-ups or email collection.
  • No ads and no third-party tracking.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

When it connects to the internet

Transcriber only uses the network to download software and models it needs, and only at these moments:

  • Speech models: when you choose to download a model, it is fetched from Hugging Face.
  • The speech engine: the first time you transcribe, uv downloads the open-source Python packages Transcriber relies on, from the Python Package Index.

Like any download, those services can see the request (for example your IP address) under their own privacy policies. No audio, transcripts or personal information are sent to them.

If you already use Handy, Transcriber reads the models Handy downloaded from your Mac. It doesn't change Handy's settings or data.

Settings stored on your Mac

The only thing Transcriber remembers is which model you last selected, stored locally in macOS's standard app preferences.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics of its own. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. The download is served from GitHub Releases.

Children

Transcriber does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Its history is public in the GitHub repository.
